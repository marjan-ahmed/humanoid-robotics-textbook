# Introduction to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive course on **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**. This textbook bridges the gap between digital intelligence and physical embodiment, providing you with the knowledge and skills needed to design, develop, and deploy autonomous humanoid robots.

## About This Course

This course integrates all aspects of humanoid robotics, from fundamental principles to advanced implementation. You'll explore the convergence of artificial intelligence, robotics, and embodied cognition through hands-on projects and theoretical foundations.

## What You'll Learn

Throughout this course, you will:
- Understand the principles of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Master ROS 2 architecture and middleware for robotic systems
- Develop simulation environments using Gazebo and Unity
- Implement NVIDIA Isaac systems for advanced robotics
- Create Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems for intelligent interaction
- Design complete autonomous humanoid systems

## Getting Started

Begin your journey by exploring the course modules through the navigation sidebar. Each chapter builds upon previous concepts, providing a structured learning path from foundational concepts to advanced applications.

## Hardware Requirements

To fully engage with this course, you'll need access to robotic hardware or simulation environments. See our [Hardware Requirements](./#hardware-requirements) section for detailed recommendations.

## Prerequisites

This course assumes familiarity with:
- Programming fundamentals (Python/C++)
- Basic understanding of linear algebra and calculus
- Introduction to robotics concepts
- Machine learning basics
